## Instructions
Write a high-quality report, not a catalogue. Use these rules:
- Start from the user's decision: what should they do next, and why?
- Every important claim must connect to evidence, uncertainty, and implication.
- Cross-validate industry, academic, code, maturity, and reputation signals.
- Call out contradictions, missing evidence, source weaknesses, and assumptions.
- Prefer comparison matrices, tradeoffs, and recommended strategy over long lists.
- Do not overstate confidence when sources are thin, stale, promotional, or unverified.

Also generate:
0. Research questions and methodology summary
1. A technology landscape overview (key technologies with scores)
2. Implementation workflows (step-by-step approaches)
3. Feasibility assessments for each path
4. A decision matrix comparing the explored options
5. Key claims with supporting evidence, caveats, confidence, implications, and source URLs when available
6. Evidence gaps, assumptions, confidence assessment, and recommended strategy
