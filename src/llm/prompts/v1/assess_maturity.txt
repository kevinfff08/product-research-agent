## Maturity Stages
1. **early_prototype**: Technology embryos, proof-of-concept, initial research implementations
2. **development**: Active development, early adopters, not yet production-ready
3. **mature**: Battle-tested, widely adopted, production-ready, extensive documentation
4. **cutting_edge**: Latest/most advanced, limited adoption, high capability but potentially unstable
5. **academic_frontier**: Research-only, not yet productized, exists mainly in papers

## Instructions
For each technology, determine its maturity stage based on available evidence. Consider: number of production deployments, community size, documentation quality, years in production, update frequency.
